# Illustrative video concepts

All six exercise demonstrations have now been generated; see [media verification](video-verification.md) for current assets and limitations. The briefs below remain early design concepts, not exact shot descriptions or evidence of a test bake. Captions are separate from the bitmap.

## Know when to stop mixing

A 16:9 close-up in a bright home kitchen: hands gently folding pale vanilla batter with a silicone spatula in a ceramic bowl. Show a few small lumps, no dry flour, a clean counter, measured ingredients nearby, and no raw batter being tasted. Natural daylight, realistic food photography, no text or logos. Suggested shot sequence: dry ingredients, wet ingredients, gentle folds, scrape the bowl, stop at just combined.

## Ready for the oven

A 16:9 overhead still of batter leveled in a parchment-lined 20 cm round cake pan on a counter. No hands touching a hot oven.

## Ready to share

A 16:9 still of a fully cooled single-layer vanilla cake with a thin layer of vanilla frosting and colorful sprinkles on a simple plate. One slice served alongside it. Natural kitchen light, approachable rather than professional pastry styling.

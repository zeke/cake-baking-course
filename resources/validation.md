## Status

Record any recipe adjustments here.

## Safety references

- [CDC: Raw flour and dough](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/foods/no-raw-dough.html): do not taste raw batter; raw flour and eggs can carry germs. Wash hands, utensils, and surfaces after handling them.
- [FDA: Handling flour safely](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/handling-flour-safely-what-you-need-know): keep raw flour away from ready-to-eat food and follow baking instructions.

Use dry oven mitts and a heat-safe landing spot. Keep children supervised near heat and knives. Check ingredient labels and guests' allergies; this default cake contains wheat, eggs, and milk. Do not claim substitutions are allergy-safe without checking every product and cross-contact.

# Preheat the oven

## Set the oven

Before heating, position a rack in the middle and remove anything stored in the oven. Set a conventional oven to **175°C (350°F)**. If yours is fan-only, pause and consult its manual rather than assuming the same setting.

## Prepare the pan

Lightly grease the 20 cm (8 inch) round pan. Line its bottom with a circle of baking parchment and lightly grease the paper. Do not use wax paper.

## Prepare a safe workspace

Put dry oven mitts and a heat-safe landing spot nearby. Keep towels and packaging away from the oven. Wash your hands and clear the counter.

## Check readiness

Wait for the oven's preheated indicator. If you have an oven thermometer, check it too. Tell your agent the temperature, oven setting, and whether the pan is ready. Keep the oven on while you mix the batter.

# Bake the cake

## Fill the pan

Pour the batter into the prepared pan and gently level the surface. Place it on the oven's middle rack with dry oven mitts. Close the door and set a timer for **25 minutes**.

## Check the center

The draft estimate is **25–35 minutes**, but ovens vary and this recipe is untested. At 25 minutes, carefully check: the surface should be set and a skewer inserted into the center should come out without wet batter. A few moist crumbs are fine. If wet batter remains, bake another 3–5 minutes and check again. Do not rely on color or the timer alone.

## Cool safely

Use dry mitts to place the pan on a heat-safe surface. Turn the oven off. Let the cake rest in the pan for about 10 minutes, then carefully loosen the edge and turn it onto a rack. Remove the parchment. Let it cool completely before frosting; this may take an hour or more.

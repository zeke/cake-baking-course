# Serve to guests

## Prepare to share

Check your guests' allergies before offering cake. Tell them it contains wheat, eggs, and milk, plus anything in the frosting or decorations. If you cannot confirm an ingredient is safe for a guest, do not promise it is.

## Cut and serve

Use a clean knife and plate. Cut into about eight wedges, adjusting portions to suit your guests. Keep children supervised around knives. If guests arrive later, cover and store the cake according to the frosting label.

## Reflect

Tell your agent whether you served it now or prepared it for later. What did you learn about mixing, and what would you change next time? A dry or dense result is useful evidence, not something to hide; note it before revising this experimental recipe.

## Tidy up

Check the oven is off. Wash bowls, utensils, and counters, and put ingredients away. Follow ingredient and frosting storage instructions for leftovers. Cleanup is encouraged but does not block course completion.

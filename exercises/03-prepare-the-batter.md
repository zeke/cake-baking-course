# Prepare the batter

## Measure the dry ingredients

In the first bowl, whisk together **200 g flour, 150 g sugar, 8 g baking powder, and 2 g salt**. Break up obvious dry clumps.

## Mix the wet ingredients

In the second bowl, whisk **2 large eggs, 120 ml milk, 80 ml neutral oil, and 5 ml vanilla** until evenly combined.

## Fold the batter

Before pouring, consider: what will tell you it is time to stop mixing? Tell your agent your prediction.

Pour the wet mixture into the dry mixture. Use a spatula to fold from the bottom over the top, turning the bowl as you go. Scrape the sides and bottom so hidden flour joins the mixture.

**Fold gently until no dry flour remains. A few small lumps are fine.** Do not keep beating just to make it perfectly smooth; extra mixing can make the cake tougher.

## Check your batter

Scrape the bottom once more. Look for dry flour pockets. If you see any, fold a few more times and check again. Do not taste raw batter. Wash hands and tools that touched raw eggs or flour.

Tell your agent what you see or say **“My batter is ready.”** If it seems unusually dry or separated, pause and review what you added. Keep this step active until you have checked it. Once ready, proceed directly to baking.

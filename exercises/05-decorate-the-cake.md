# Decorate the cake

## Check it is cool

Touch the outside gently with clean hands. The cake must be completely cool before frosting or the frosting can melt and slide. If it is warm, wait.

## Choose your finish

Use the ready-made vanilla frosting and optional sprinkles from your ingredient list. Read the label for allergens and storage instructions. Pick a simple pattern, a few swirls, or a sprinkle border.

## Spread and decorate

Place the cake on a plate. Spread a thin layer of frosting across the top with a spatula, working gently to avoid tearing it. Add more until covered, using roughly 200 g or as much as you prefer. Add your chosen decoration.

## Share your result

Describe your finish to your agent, or optionally share a photo. A photo is not required. Explain one choice you made. Keep the cake stored according to the frosting label until serving.
